Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information 3minsto9 Operations ("we", "us") collects through 3minsto9.co.uk, why, and what you can do about it. We are an independent studio based in the UK and the data controller for this information. The in-world pages of our game (such as the MLTK and Team Rabbit pages) are fiction; this policy is the real one and covers the whole site.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up on the site (for example through the terminal you reach from the zine's QR code, a newsletter form, or the contact page), along with which form you used and the date and time.
  • Anything you send us if you email us, such as your name and the contents of your message.
  • Email activity: our email provider records whether our emails are delivered, opened or clicked, so we can tell whether they are working.
  • Technical information: like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request details (such as the page and browser type) when you visit. We use IP addresses briefly to stop abuse, for example to limit repeated sign-up or password attempts.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. We only use:

  • zine_access: remembers for 30 days that you have entered the serial from the printed zine, so the downloads stay unlocked. It contains no personal information.
  • admin_session: used only when our staff sign in to manage the site.
  • Browser storage for your light/dark theme choice and small pieces of game progress. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Sending you emails (game dispatches, puzzle drops, and news about our releases and crowdfunding) because you asked us to. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email.
  • Replying to messages you send us, and running and securing the website. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating the studio and its website.

We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

4. Who Processes Your Information

We use a small number of service providers who handle data on our behalf:

  • Render: hosts the website (servers in the EU).
  • Upstash: stores the list of email sign-ups.
  • MailerLite: sends our emails and manages unsubscribes.
  • Proton: our email inbox, if you write to us.
  • Sentry: reports website errors to us, with personal data collection turned off.
  • Google Fonts: supplies the fonts on some pages, so your browser contacts Google's servers.

If you buy from our shop, the purchase is handled by our storefront provider under its own privacy policy. Some providers may process data outside the UK; where they do, they rely on approved safeguards such as UK-approved data transfer agreements.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep your email address while you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, our email provider keeps a record of that so you are not emailed again. You can ask us at any time to delete your address and sign-up details completely. Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need them to deal with your message.

6. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, or ask for it in a portable format. You can withdraw consent to emails at any time. Email us using the details below and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Data Security

We use HTTPS throughout the site, restrict access to our systems, and choose providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy question or request, email hello@3minsto9.co.uk or write to 3minsto9 Operations, Unit 176418, PO Box 7169, Poole, BH15 9EL, United Kingdom.
